
# Saurabh Kumar, PhD

**Postdoctoral Research Associate · Flaum Eye Institute, University of Rochester Medical Center**
iPSC & mouse models of retinal degeneration · CLN3 (Batten) disease · AMD · Retinal lipid metabolism · Reproducible analysis in R

My scientific journey is driven by a desire to understand the fundamental biology of retinal diseases and translate that understanding into meaningful interventions. During my doctoral research, I used multi-omics to uncover how altered lipid metabolism, specifically arachidonic acid pathways, drives neurodegeneration in the developing retina. This work also led to my role in co-developing non-invasive, tear-based biosensors for early ocular disease detection.

As a postdoctoral researcher in Dr. Ruchira Singh's laboratory, I bring expertise in retinal lipidomics, cellular trafficking, lysosomal exocytosis and cellular stress to study RPE biology in AMD and CLN3 disease. I study how CLN3 mutation leads to mis-trafficking of lysosomal enzymes and altered lysosomal pH, and, using iPSC-derived RPE and in vivo models that mimic drusen formation, how lipid mediators such as PLA2G2A and lysosomal dysfunction trigger early RPE dysfunction. My aim is to bridge basic retinal metabolism with advanced disease modeling to uncover early, targeted therapies that reduce the burden of macular degeneration.

This GitHub holds the R code behind my publications, so the analyses can be checked and reused.

### 🔬 Research focus
- **CLN3 disease (juvenile Batten disease)** — lysosomal enzyme trafficking, lysosomal pH and exocytosis
- **Age-related macular degeneration** — drusen-forming iPSC-RPE and mouse models; PLA2G2A and lysosomal dysfunction
- **Retinopathy of prematurity** — arachidonic acid and prostaglandin metabolism, tear biomarkers
- **Omics** — LC-MS lipidomics and multi-omics of vitreous, plasma and tear
- **Reproducible analysis** — version-controlled R pipelines that accompany each paper
