Saurabh Kumar, PhD
Postdoctoral Researcher · Flaum Eye Institute, University of Rochester Medical Center
Retinal lipid metabolism · AMD & iPSC-RPE disease modeling · Retinopathy of prematurity · Reproducible analysis in R for biomedical research

My scientific work is driven by a desire to understand the fundamental biology of retinal diseases and to translate 
that understanding into meaningful interventions. During my doctoral research, I used multi-omics to uncover how 
altered lipid metabolism- specifically arachidonic acid pathways- drives neurodegeneration in the developing retina. 
This foundational work led to my role in co-developing non-invasive, tear-based biosensors for early ocular disease 
detection. As a postdoctoral researcher in Dr. Ruchira Singh’s laboratory, I now apply my expertise in retinal 
lipidomics and cellular stress to study RPE biology in AMD. By engineering hiPSC and in vivo models that 
faithfully mimic hallmark pathology such as drusen formation, I am dissecting how lipid mediators such as 
PLA2G2A and lysosomal dysfunction trigger early RPE dysfunction. My contribution lies in bridging basic retinal 
metabolism with advanced disease modeling to uncover early, targeted therapies that reduce the burden of macular 
degeneration. This GitHub holds the R code behind my publications, so the analyses can be checked and reused.
